What Do the Hockey Stats Mean?

Click any term to open it. This is useful while you learn both hockey and HTML.

PTS — Standings Points

An NHL team normally earns 2 points for a win and 1 point for an overtime or shootout loss.

GF / GA / DIFF

GF is goals scored, GA is goals allowed, and goal differential is GF minus GA. Positive is generally better.

PTS% — Points Percentage

Points earned divided by the maximum possible points. It helps compare teams that have played different numbers of games.

G / A / PTS

For skaters: goals, assists, and total points. A player's points equal goals plus assists.

+/- — Plus/Minus

A skater gets a plus when on the ice for many even-strength or shorthanded goals for, and a minus for many goals against. It is a context stat, not a complete measure of defense.

PIM / PPG / SHG / GWG

PIM = penalty minutes, PPG = power-play goals, SHG = shorthanded goals, and GWG = game-winning goals.

SOG / SH%

SOG is shots on goal. Shooting percentage is goals divided by shots on goal, multiplied by 100.
